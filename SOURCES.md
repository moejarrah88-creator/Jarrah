## Limits and discrepancies

- The brochure does not provide complete ingredient lists, measured nutrition panels, caffeine quantities, clinical evidence, product pricing, live stock, or a technical specification. No values are invented to fill those gaps.
- Page 11 labels a variant “Sugar Free”, but its pictured can reads “Low Sugar”. Catalog names follow slide headings and the website explicitly flags the conflict.
- “Pinky Grip Fruit” follows the spelling printed on page 15; it has not been silently changed to another fruit name.
- Package volumes vary or are not reliably legible across images. No universal serving size is asserted.
- Locations are those listed in the corporate brochure, not confirmed shops, delivery destinations, or street-level office addresses.
- This source is a presentation, not proof of current commercial availability. The website offers contact and source links rather than a checkout.
